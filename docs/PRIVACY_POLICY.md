Last updated: 26 September 2026

WealthFlow ("the app", "we") is a personal finance app for Android, operated by
Salvino Madison, an individual developer. Contact: salvinokevin7@gmail.com.

## What stays on your phone

Your bank statement PDFs, the transactions read from them, your bank accounts,
budgets and insights are processed and stored **only on your device**. They are
not uploaded to us or anyone else. No AI or machine-learning service reads them:
transactions are sorted by fixed rules, on your device. If you delete the app or
switch phones, this data is gone; importing your statements again restores it.

## What we store on our servers

When you sign in we keep:

- **Sign-in details** from Google: your account identifier and email address.
- **Your profile**, as you enter it: name, email, phone number, age range,
  monthly income range, main goal and occupation.
- **Your categories and rules**: category names, colours and monthly budgets,
  and the merchant patterns and amount thresholds you write to sort transactions.

This is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, in India (Mumbai region).
Each user can read and change only their own records.

## How we use it

To sign you in, personalise the app, and restore your categories and rules on a
new phone. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it for
analytics. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

## Who else is involved

- **Google**, for sign-in (subject to Google's privacy policy).
- **Supabase**, which hosts the account database on our behalf.

We do not share your data with anyone else, unless required by law.

## Your choices

- Edit your profile at any time in the app (Menu > Profile).
- **Wipe all data** (Menu > Profile) removes everything on your phone and your
  synced rules, but keeps your account.
- **Delete account** (Menu > Profile) removes your account, as described below.

## Deleting your account

In the app, go to Menu > Profile > **Delete account**, type DELETE and confirm.
This immediately and permanently removes your account, profile, categories and
rules from our servers, and everything stored on your device.

If you can't open the app, email salvinokevin7@gmail.com from the address on your
account with the subject "Delete my WealthFlow account". We will delete the
server data within 30 days and confirm by reply. Uninstalling the app removes the
data on that phone.

We keep server data only until you delete your account.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to server data is
restricted so each user can reach only their own records.

## Children

WealthFlow is for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.
